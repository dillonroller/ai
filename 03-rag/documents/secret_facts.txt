Dillon believes that life itself is computation. Not a metaphor — literally. Every
cell, every thought, every interaction between particles is information being
processed. The universe doesn't just contain computers. It is one. Consciousness,
evolution, gravity — all of it is computation running on the fabric of reality.
Programming isn't something humans invented. It's something we discovered. We just
happen to be the first part of the universe that can write it down.
